What the system is lacking now

T1The code block sits behind two prose blocksruns 17, 21

Rules and design before code was right for a non-reasoning model. A thinking model reads in its reasoning tokens; the visible prose is a second pass before the first line of code, and a cut-off at the cap then salvages nothing.

Change: code first, examples second, then short rules, design and traps. In batch 10.

T2Oracle and stress authors need a sub-minute modelrun 21

qwen was dropped for being wrong, not slow; Opus at low effort was right but took 155 s, starting preparation late and filling the concurrency cap.

Change: Sonnet 5 at low effort in the fast role for both authors. In batch 10.

T3Solve latency variance against a fixed cap3 of 10 cut

Ten Opus solves at medium effort ran 152–240+ s; three did not finish by their cap.

Change: T1 makes a cut recoverable; if the cut rate stays high, lower the solve effort and measure correctness.

T4Worst-case timing coverage is the author's guessrun 20

The legal 9.8 MB input ran in 0.38 s; the adjudicator's in-bounds worst case for the same candidate ran in 5.2–6.3 s against our 5 s limit.

Not addressed: the judge's limit is unknown; EXPECTED_MAX catches inputs that stop early, not shapes that are merely easy.

T5A bounds check from a weak model is unsoundrun 20

The qwen bounds() omitted descriptor nodes and nesting; the input happened to be legal.

Change: a stronger author (T2); cross-check bounds() against validate() on the small tier if spurious verdicts appear.

Model capability, stated plainly

claude-opus-5, medium effort, as the solver: three candidates, all correct on 3,200–12,632 inputs, asymptotically right each time. 152–240+ s, about $0.40 per attempt. No longer the problem on this statement.

claude-opus-5, low effort, as the stress author: valid edges and legal, large, 1018-bearing max inputs in three of three runs, one with an off-by-one that ended processing early, now detected by EXPECTED_MAX. 70–79 s. As the oracle author: 155 s, too slow for the overlap.

qwen3-coder as the oracle author: constant answers, precondition checks off by one, no array path, a regenerated replacement with a syntax error. Retired from the role.
