What the system is lacking now, ranked by what it cost on the focus problem

S1An unjudged max-size input is the only timing evidencebench15

The stress author defined a nesting cap of 60 and never used it; the input nested 200,000 deep. The validator is the literal reference, which cannot finish at max size, so it gave no verdict and the harness used the input undegraded. Both candidates were killed on an input the statement forbids; the correct one was emitted only by tie-break, and the other is 10× slower on legal maxima.

Change: no validate verdict means degraded stress evidence, never a failure; gen_max must assert every stated bound before returning; when two candidates pass, the faster measured one wins.

S2Examples spelled with arithmetic are droppedbench15

ast.literal_eval rejects 10**18, so the two examples covering the 1018 trap were lost and c1 kept three small ones.

Change: evaluate example lines with a restricted AST that allows integer arithmetic over constants.

S3Gating waits for the slowest attempt43 s, 31 s

The orchestrator joins on all attempts before gating any. With a thinking model the second attempt can be 40 s behind or hit the cap.

Change: gate each candidate as its reply arrives.

S4The repair call cannot fit a thinking modelbench16 1c18

The repair cap is 25 % of usable, tuned to a 12 s model; against a 168 s role it is a guaranteed timeout.

Change: per-role reasoning effort for repairs and fresh solves (low), a cap that follows the role's measured latency, and a repair that cannot fit is skipped rather than started.

S5Two candidates against one oracle is still not a signalbench16 1dea

Both candidates agreed byte-for-byte against the oracle on the failing input, a hand-traced example contradicted the oracle on the same rule, and the errored repair call's fallback verdict was candidate.

Change: identical candidate outputs on the failing case count toward the oracle dispute; an errored repair call yields no verdict.

S6The examples author's inputs can be out of contract5 of 18

Handled since batch 4 (rejections are not disagreements), but each rejected example is a lost check.

Change: one sentence in the examples block: satisfy every stated precondition and spell containers exactly as the contract says.

Model capability, stated plainly

anthropic/claude-opus-5, medium effort: correct semantics and intended complexity on 5cb2 and 1dea, and the right reading plus one sentinel bug on 1c18, where every earlier model misread the statement. 150–200 s per solve, $0.30–0.40 per attempt. This is the model to keep in the strong role.

qwen/qwen3-coder as the oracle author: 12–30 s per call and usable on 5cb2, but wrong on 1c18 (two misreads, every counterexample a phantom) and on 1dea (one wrong conjunct). The oracle is now the weaker author, which is what S5 exists for.

The OpenRouter key's total limit was hit at $1.13 into the four-problem batch; its last two rows are key failures, not solver results.
